Teaching

Computer science at Mines Saint-Étienne, for engineering students of the Ingénieur civil des mines programme and master's students. Most of it in English.

Programme responsibilities

  1. 2027–
    DAKAI, Data, Agents, Knowledge in Artificial Intelligence, master's track of the Master Informatique, operated by Université Jean Monnet and Mines Saint-Étienne. It replaces CPS2 from September 2027. Preparation of the curriculum and of the accreditation.
  2. 2021–
    CPS2, Cyber-Physical and Social Systems, M1 and M2 taught in English. Co-coordinator with Pierre Maret: timetables, admissions, curriculum redesign for the 2022 accreditation.
  3. 2019–
    Computer science major (M-INFO) of the Ingénieur civil des mines programme, 160 hours. Head of the teaching group; redesign of the computer science electives in 2023.

Courses

  • Technological foundations of software development (M1)

Engineering programme

  • Computer science major: Internet of Things, Semantic Web, cloud computing
  • Toolbox: web, mobile and embedded programming
  • AI challenge, AI Society: Issues, Impacts and Applications

Other institutions

  • IMT Atlantique, M2: Data and digitalisation of energy
  • ISTP, apprentice engineers in energy: Smart grids

Teaching methods include project- and problem-based learning, flipped classes, and a serious game to learn Scrum. Courses are being adapted to generative AI with colleagues.
